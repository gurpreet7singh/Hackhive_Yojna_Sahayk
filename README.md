# Hackhive_Yojna_Sahayk
Yojana Sahayak

AI-Powered Government Scheme Assistant

Yojana Sahayak is a multilingual AI-powered chatbot designed to help citizens easily discover, understand, and access government welfare schemes. Built during a 36-hour hackathon, the platform leverages Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to provide accurate, context-aware responses grounded in official government documents.

🚀 Features

AI-Powered Assistance: Ask questions about government schemes in natural language.

Multilingual Support: Interact in English and Hindi.

RAG-Based Responses: Retrieves relevant information from official documents to generate grounded answers.

Scheme Information: Explore government schemes, benefits, and guidelines.

Eligibility Assistance: Helps users understand scheme eligibility requirements.

Official Document Access: Access government guidelines and FAQs.

User Authentication: Registration and login functionality.

Conversation History: Save, revisit, rename, and delete chat conversations.

Responsive Interface: User-friendly design across devices.

🧠 How It Works

User submits a question in English or Hindi.

The retrieval system searches relevant document chunks from the knowledge base.

Multilingual embeddings help identify semantically relevant information.

Retrieved context is passed to Google Gemini.

Gemini generates a context-aware response grounded in the retrieved information.

The response and relevant sources are presented to the user.
